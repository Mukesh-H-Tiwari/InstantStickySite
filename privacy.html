Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 07, 2026

At Instant Sticky, accessible from our mobile application, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Instant Sticky and how we use it. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. "Local-First" Data Policy

Instant Sticky is strictly a "Local-First" application. This means:

  • No Server Storage: Your notes, ideas, and preferences are stored exclusively on your own mobile device. We do not have servers that store your note content.
  • No Account Required: You can use 100% of the app's features without ever creating an account or providing an email address.

2. In-App Purchases

We use Google Play Billing for in-app purchases (e.g., unlocking premium themes).

  • Billing Data: All payment information is processed by Google. We do not see or store your credit card details or billing address.
  • Purchase History: We only receive a confirmation token from Google to verify that the unlock was successful.

3. Device Permissions

To function correctly, the app requests specific technical permissions:

  • Storage/Preferences: To save your notes and settings on your device.
  • Widget Management: To display and update the sticky notes on your home screen.

4. Data Security

Because your data is stored locally, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using standard Android security features (pin, pattern, or biometric lock) to protect your device from unauthorized access.

5. Children's Information

Instant Sticky does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any data at all, it is safe for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@bytecode.com.
